Accessibility

What we have done to make this site usable by everyone, what still is not right, and how to tell us about it.

Conformance

This site is built to meet WCAG 2.1 level AA. That is a standard, not a slogan: it covers colour contrast, keyboard operation, text alternatives, form labels, headings and the structure of tables. The site was checked against it page by page.

Display settings

The bar at the top of every page opens a panel with four controls. Whatever you choose is remembered on this device and applied to every page before it appears, so you never see the default first.

  • Text size — three steps. Everything on the page scales together, including tables.
  • Colours — normal, high contrast, or white on black.
  • Letter spacing — wider spacing between letters, words and lines.
  • Images — decorative artwork can be switched off. Nothing that carries information is an image.

Using the site with a keyboard

  • Tab moves forward, Shift + Tab back. The focused element always has a visible outline.
  • The first stop on every page is Skip to content, which jumps past the navigation.
  • Escape closes the display-settings panel.
  • Tables scroll sideways with the arrow keys once focused.

What we know is not right yet

Publishing this list is part of the standard, and pretending it is empty helps nobody.

  • Some documents in the register are scans rather than text, so a screen reader cannot read them. We are replacing them; ask for any of them in another format meanwhile.
  • The timetable is a wide table. It scrolls on a phone, which works but is not comfortable. A stacked mobile view is planned for the summer.
  • Video from school events has no captions before 2025.

Documents in another format

Any document from the register can be supplied as large print, plain text or an audio recording within five working days. Ask at the office, by telephone or by email. You do not have to explain why.

Reporting a problem

If something on this site is unusable for you, tell us and we will fix it. Write to access@riverside.example.edu or telephone the office on +1 (555) 011-7400. We reply within five working days and say what we are going to do.

If the answer does not satisfy you, the complaints procedure in the document register explains where to take it next.
