Admissions

The rules are the same for everyone and published in full. If anything below is unclear, the office will explain it — that is what the office is for.

1. Bring or send the documents

Completed application form, the child's birth certificate, proof of address and a medical card. Scans by email are accepted.

2. We reply within three days

You get written confirmation that the application was received, and the number it was registered under.

3. A conversation, not an exam

For Years 5 and above there is a short meeting with the deputy principal — to place the child correctly, not to filter anyone out.

  • Step-free entrance from the car park, with a call button at 90 cm.
  • Lift to all three floors of the main building.
  • Accessible toilet on the ground floor, next to the office.
  • Two marked parking spaces by the main entrance.
  • Induction loop in the assembly hall and in the office.
